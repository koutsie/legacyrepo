MusicImporter

MusicImporter 2.0.12+2

Import audio, video and image files into the Music, Videos and Photos libraries directly, without Gremlin. Drop files into /var/mobile/Media/Downloads/MusicImporter/ via iFunBox, iMazing or ifuse, or the web uploader, then tap the Import All button. Includes a CLI tool. Images need iOS 8 or later.
